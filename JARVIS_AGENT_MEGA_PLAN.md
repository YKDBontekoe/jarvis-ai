## Checkpoint 5: A5 meeting prep, A6 debrief, F22 first slice

- **Contract.** `meetings` table (owner, title, start, project, `document` jsonb; migration `AddMeetings`). The document holds participants, agenda lines (each `shared` or private), private notes, the prep pack, the debrief text and the candidates.
- **Prep (`MeetingPrepBuilder`, pure).** From the owner's own records only: project outcome and next step (A2), open accepted promises with the participants (overdue first, capped), unresolved decisions that mention a participant or the project, project files, what earlier meetings with the same people or project left unanswered or unreviewed. Every fact has a `ref`; anything missing becomes a question (template-specific for one-on-one, project update, interview, decision). The agenda is only filled when empty, so a refresh never overwrites the owner's agenda.
- **Debrief (`MeetingDebriefExtractor`, pure, rule-based, English and Dutch).** Decisions ("we decided", "besloten"), what I owe ("I'll", "ik stuur"), what a named participant will do, and questions. Dates: ISO, today/tomorrow, weekdays, day-month in both languages; a vague date ("next week", "end of the month") is kept as wording with no day. Ids are stable per kind and sentence, so a second read keeps earlier choices. Text is untrusted data: a hidden instruction produces nothing, and an injected promise is only a pending proposal (tested).
- **Acceptance.** `AcceptAsync` calls `ICommitmentService.CreateAsync` (source `meeting`) or `IDecisionService.CreateAsync` (the owner must give confidence and a review day); accepting twice creates one record; a question cannot be accepted; an accepted candidate cannot be dismissed; dismissing changes nothing but the candidate.
- **Follow-up.** Built only from accepted items and shared agenda lines; private notes, private lines and unaccepted items are excluded; `sent` is always false and there is no send endpoint.
- **Agent.** Tools `PrepareMeeting`, `DebriefMeeting`, `DraftMeetingFollowUp` (group `meetings`); the debrief tool only lists proposals and tells the user to accept in the app.
- **App.** Meetings list with calendar events, creation sheet, detail screen as described in the architecture doc.
- **Decision.** The debrief uses rules instead of a model so the demo is deterministic and works with no provider; a model reading remains a later addition behind the same candidate contract. Meetings are not yet an `EntityType`, so a meeting is reached from the Meetings screen, quick commands and prep refs (`meeting:id`), not search.
- **Tests.** Unit 1781 pass (`MeetingExtractionTests` 24, `MeetingServiceTests` 12); Dart `meetings_test` (11), full `flutter test` 761 pass, analyze clean. Real stack: `meeting_flow` (prep from seeded records, agenda kept on refresh, debrief creates nothing, accept once, edited acceptance, decision needs confidence, dismiss, unsent follow-up without private text, bad input) plus all earlier flows. Screenshots: `build/screenshots/audit-{phone,small,large-text}-meeting:m1.png`.
- **Not done.** Calendar write-back, recording or transcription, calendar-event identity beyond title and start (F22.1 stable link), proposed time blocks for accepted follow-through (F22.7), people-page open loops (F22.8 beyond the prep carry-forward), a model-based extraction, search for meetings.

## Checkpoint 6: A10 weekly review follow-through

- **What.** `WeekFollowThroughBuilder` (pure) counts the week in the owner's local Monday–Sunday from the actual records: accepted promises fulfilled or dropped that week (by local completion day), overdue and a-week-or-more overdue, decisions due next week, active projects with no next step or a finished one. Items behind each count are listed and capped. At most three suggestions, in a fixed order: give the most overdue "I owe" promise a new day, choose a project's next step, then the second overdue promise.

Dismissed suggestions are kept in the owner settings section `weekly-review.followthrough` for 28 days.
- **App.** A follow-through card on the weekly review (for the selected week): counts, items that open the real record, a quiet week says so, suggestions with the day or note chosen by the owner, "Not now".
- **Tool.** `GetWeekFollowThrough` (read-only).

## Checkpoint 9: F23 goals, weekly plan, check-ins and experiments

- **What.** Goals live in owner settings (`goals`; no migration). A goal has an outcome, motivation, optional horizon and measurable target, a check-in rhythm (3–60 days) and the minutes a week the owner can spare. It links habits (≤ 5), one project and promises (≤ 8) by id, validated against the owner, and copies nothing.
- **Weekly plan.** `GoalLogic.Propose` (pure) offers at most three steps in a fixed order — the project's open next action, linked open promises due by Sunday, remaining habit sessions — fitted to the weekly minutes; what does not fit is counted, not squeezed in. The owner accepts, shrinks, declines (not offered again for 28 days) or marks done; "Add to today" sends an accepted step to the day planner (a promise step keeps its `commitment:` source, so F05 dedupes) and so respects working hours. Proposing schedules nothing.
- **Check-ins.** Owner words only (progress required; friction, adjustment optional; on track / slipping / stuck). A missing check-in is only "due"; there is no reminder, escalation or streak loss. Habit streaks are untouched; progress shows measured values, the owner's reflections and the suggestion as three labelled blocks.
- **Keep / change / stop.** Rule-based on the last three check-ins of the current season (needs two; stuck twice, or stuck/slipping twice, → change; all on track → keep; a passed horizon → finish or move it). "Not now" keeps it quiet for 28 days; changing status or horizon clears the dismissal. Shown on the goal, in the list and in the weekly review (`GoalsReviewCard`).
- **Experiments.** One running at a time, 3–60 days, expected benefit, dated notes (observations, no causal claims), keep or stop with a conclusion.
- **Pause / archive / season.** Pause stops planning and suggestions; archive hides from the list but keeps everything; a new season restarts progress and plan while the check-in history stays.
- **Agent.** `GetGoals`, `AddGoalCheckIn`, `ProposeGoalPlan` (group `goals`); the plan tool only proposes.
- **Tests.** Unit `GoalTests` (28); Dart `goals_test` (12) and the unchanged weekly review tests; real stack `goal_flow` (links, refusal of foreign ids, check-in, plan within 90 minutes that schedules nothing, accept/shrink/schedule onto Today with the promise link, decline stays quiet, experiment, pause/reset/archive). Screenshots `build/screenshots/audit-{phone,small,large-text}-goals.png` and `-goal:g1.png` (a large-text layout bug in the check-in prompt was found from the screenshot and fixed).
- **Not done.** Using real free time instead of the owner-set weekly minutes (F23.3 asks for "actual available time"), a measurable-target progress number (the target is text), model-written reflections, medical/health framing is deliberately absent, a reminder for due check-ins (deliberately none), goal search/entity type.

## Checkpoint 11: F12.1.2 reversible statement imports

- **What.** A committed import that added rows is recorded as a batch (owner settings `finance.imports`, latest 20; ids of the rows it created). `GET /finance/imports` shows how many of a batch's rows are still there and how many were edited since; `POST /finance/imports/{id}/undo` deletes only rows the batch created that still exist and have `UpdatedAt == CreatedAt`. Edited rows, rows that were duplicates of existing ones, and unrelated rows with the same merchant stay; a second undo does nothing more; a stranger gets 404. Importing the same file twice already gave one result (duplicates are skipped) and now also creates no second batch.
- **App.** Budgets tab: up to three recent batches with Undo (confirm first, then "Removed N rows, kept M you edited").
- **Tests.** Unit `FinanceTests` (+4), Dart `finance_screen_test` (+1); real stack `import_undo_flow` (preview has no batch, double commit = one result, edit + unrelated manual row, undo removes only the untouched row, idempotent, stranger 404).

## Checkpoint 13: A12 interactive scenario

- **What.** Two deterministic scenarios in `ScenarioCalculator` (pure, decimal): a cost (current monthly cost, alternative monthly cost, months, one-time switching cost, one currency) and a pace (weekly hours, task hours, buffer percent).
